01 / About

From resource landscapes to living cities.

I study the spatial and ecological transformation of cities and regions shaped by resource extraction, industrial change and urbanisation.

My academic work combines quantitative spatial analysis with urban and regional planning. I am particularly interested in resource-based cities, mining regions, urban morphology, land-use change, ecological restoration, labour reallocation and the transition from extractive economies toward more diversified and resilient urban systems.
